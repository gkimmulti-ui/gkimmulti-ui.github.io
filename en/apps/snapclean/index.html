[FULL DESCRIPTION] 2473/4000 characters Snap Clean finds the photos and videos quietly filling up your phone — duplicates, near-identical burst shots, screenshots you never opened again, blurry misfires and oversized files — and helps you clear them in a few taps. Every scan runs on your device. WHAT SNAP CLEAN FINDS • Duplicates — identical copies, matched by file hash • Similar Photos — burst shots and near-identical frames, grouped together • Screenshots — the ones you saved once and forgot about • Blurry Photos — out-of-focus shots, detected automatically • Large Photos and Large Videos — your biggest files, sorted by size BEST SHOT KEEPS THE ONE YOU WANT In every group of similar photos, Snap Clean marks the sharpest frame as Best Shot and leaves it unselected. The keeper stays, the rest go. CLEAN THE WAY YOU PREFER • Smart Clean — one scan across every category • Review grid — see everything before it goes and tap to change any selection • Swipe Clean — swipe left to queue for deletion, right to keep, one photo at a time COMPRESS INSTEAD OF DELETING Not ready to lose a photo? Compress it instead. Choose High Quality, Recommended or Maximum Saving, and Snap Clean saves a smaller copy. Your original stays untouched until you decide to remove it yourself. NOTHING IS DELETED BY ACCIDENT Snap Clean never removes anything on its own. You select the files, you confirm in the app, and Android asks you once more before anything is deleted. PRIVATE BY DESIGN Your photos are analyzed entirely on your device. Images, thumbnails, file names, EXIF metadata, location data and file hashes are never uploaded — we do not operate a server that receives them. SNAP CLEAN PRO Without PRO you can clean up to 50 photos in a session, and unlock 100 more by watching a short ad. The free version is supported by ads. PRO removes the limits: • Unlimited cleaning • Smart similar detection and Best Shot • Photo and video compression • No ads PRO is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Google Play account settings. PERMISSIONS Snap Clean needs photo library access to scan and manage your media. You can grant full access or select only certain photos — the app works either way. LANGUAGES English, 한국어, 日本語, 简体中文, Русский, Filipino Questions or feedback: gkimmulti@gmail.com
