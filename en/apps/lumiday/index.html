One line a day. A little space for you.

Start with a daily prompt from a collection of 500 questions. Record your mood, weather, tags, and up to five photos, then decorate entries with stickers and pastel themes. Build a journaling habit with a character that grows as you write, and revisit your days through calendars and mood graphs. No account is needed: entries and photos stay on your device, with PIN protection and manual backups.

  • 500 writing prompts
  • Photos & mood tracking
  • A growing companion
  • PIN lock & backup
