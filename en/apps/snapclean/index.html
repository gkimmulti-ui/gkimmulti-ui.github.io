Keep the memories. Make some room.

Find duplicate and similar photos, screenshots, blurry pictures, and large files to tidy your gallery. Photos are analyzed on your device, and you review and confirm files before deletion. Scan categories with Smart Clean, sort one image at a time with Swipe Clean, or keep compressed copies of photos you want to save.

  • Duplicate & similar photos
  • On-device analysis
  • Swipe cleanup
  • Review before deleting
Good to know

The free version includes ads and cleanup limits. Some features, including smart similar-photo detection, Best Shot, photo and video compression, and ad removal, require PRO. See the app and Google Play for details.
