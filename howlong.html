Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

1. In short

HowLong? has no accounts and no server of ours. We do not collect, store or transmit your personal information.

2. What stays on your device

The dates you save (title, date, emoji) and your app settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us, and removing the app removes them.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process device identifiers such as the advertising ID, along with coarse device and usage data. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy, and you can limit personalised ads in your device's advertising settings.

4. Sharing

When you share a card, the image and text are handed to the app you pick (a messenger, mail app and so on). What happens to it after that is covered by that app's own policy.

5. Permissions

The app needs internet access to load ads. It does not ask for contacts, photos, location or the camera.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your choices

You can delete individual saved dates in My dates, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here in the app. For any question about privacy, use Settings and then Contact us. You can also write to us directly at gkimmulti@gmail.com.
