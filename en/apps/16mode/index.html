    Google Play · Good to know

    MBTI 16MODE reimagines personality types not as fixed labels but as 16 "modes" (abilities) you can awaken and level up. ■ 16 Modes From Strategist, Thinker and Commander to Artist, Adventurer and Entertainer — awaken the MBTI mode that fits your moment. Tell the app what you need right now (focus, decision, empathy…) and it recommends the best mode. ■ Grow through real quests Complete small real-life missions like "speak up once in a meeting" or "send that overdue message" to train 8 abilities: Decisiveness, Action, Confidence, Empathy, Expression, Focus, Sociability and Planning. ■ See your growth A skill map, a growth-balance radar and a weekly report show how you change over time. Streaks and badges keep you going. ■ Offline-first, privacy-friendly No sign-up required. Everything is stored only on your device — no personal data is sent to any server. ■ 6 languages English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Filipino. Note: MBTI here is reference for self-understanding and recommendations, not a diagnosis. A higher level means more experience, not a better person.
