Ttirang: Zodiac Friends

Meet 36 friends inspired by the 12 zodiac animals, enjoy screen walks, small missions and hide-and-seek, and keep a diary.

    Google Play · Good to know

    Invite a little friend into your day. Ttirang brings you 36 characters inspired by the 12 animals of the East Asian zodiac. Choose the animal of your birth year or simply a friend you love. Find your favorite Meet the rat, ox, tiger, rabbit, dragon, snake, horse, sheep, monkey, rooster, dog and pig, with three characters for each animal. Feed your friend, play together and discover different expressions and reactions. A companion on your screen Turn on the companion feature to let your friend walk over other apps and greet you with short speech bubbles. This Android feature requires permission to display over other apps. Little missions and hide-and-seek Spend a moment on daily missions or find a friend who is hiding. Make a little room for play in your everyday routine. Your daily diary Write about your mood and the moments you shared. Diary entries are stored on your device. Export and import your diary to keep your own copy. Ttirang supports 10 languages. Make each day your own with a little zodiac friend. Kenneth App gkimmulti@gmail.com https://gkimmulti-ui.github.io/
