MBTI 16MODE

Self-growth · Gamified

↗↗↗↗
Swipe to browse · tap to enlarge

Small quests. New possibilities.

A gamified self-growth app that treats personality types not as fixed labels but as 16 modes you can switch on when you need them. Tell it what you need right now — focus, decisiveness, empathy — and it suggests the mode that fits, then hands you small real-life quests like "speak up once in today's meeting" to build eight different strengths. Track progress with a skill map, growth radar, and weekly report. No account needed; everything stays on your device.

  • 16 modes
  • Real-life quests
  • Growth reports
  • No account needed
Good to know

MBTI here is reference material for self-understanding and suggestions, not a diagnosis. A higher level does not mean a better person — it reflects usage experience.
