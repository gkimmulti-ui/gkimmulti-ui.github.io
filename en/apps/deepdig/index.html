    Google Play · Good to know

    Every tap takes your mining adventure a little deeper. Deep Dig is a single-player mining game about collecting minerals, improving your equipment and unlocking new mines. Tap to mine, recruit a crew and use automatic mining to keep your expedition moving. 100 mines across 10 regions Explore forests, icy landscapes, deserts, lava fields and ancient ruins. Level up and clear each mine to unlock the next. Face a guardian every 10 mines as you work toward the final challenge. Mine, collect and grow stronger Discover a variety of minerals and regional crafting materials, and fill your mineral collection. Find 72 pieces of equipment, including pickaxes, drills and charms. Craft and upgrade your gear to improve your mining power. Tap mining and automatic mining Enjoy hands-on mining with the convenience of automatic production. Hire and train fellow miners, then improve your power, speed, bag capacity and other abilities to support your next expedition. Automatic mining runs while you play. 5 characters, 15 looks Choose a character with bonuses that suit your play style and customize their appearance with 15 looks in total. Try different combinations of characters and equipment as you progress. More goals along the way Complete quests, collect daily check-in rewards and open treasure chests. Fill your collection, unlock the next mine and push your own exploration records further. Play offline in your language No account is required. Play offline with progress saved automatically on your device. Choose Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Russian or Filipino in Settings. Go deeper at your own pace in Deep Dig.
