Moonmori: Night Market

Sell food beneath the moonlight, open new stalls and grow your pixel-art night market with in-game coins.

    Google Play · Good to know

    Start with a little stall beneath the moonlight. Moonmori: Night Market is a 2D pixel-art tycoon game where you sell food, earn in-game coins and grow your market. 24 stalls and 120 dishes Open stalls for skewers, street snacks, sushi, burgers, desserts and drinks. Upgrade each stall to unlock recipes, cook faster and raise selling prices. A lively market full of little moments Meet 8 guest types and animated cooks at every stall. Orders, cooking smoke, flames, food deliveries and sale effects bring your market to life. VIP guests pay double. Build at your own pace Browse dishes and sales in the food book. Hold a star festival for a temporary increase in visitors and selling prices. Return to collect up to 2 hours of offline coin earnings. Coins are virtual currency for use within the game. Play in 10 languages Choose Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified or Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German or Indonesian. Adjust language, sound and decorative effects in Settings. Progress and settings are stored on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove your saved progress. A closer look at your market Tap a stall to open its cooking view. Plan your next opening on the expansion screen across 4 districts, and spend in-game coins on decorations that give your market its own character. Optional ads Ads are shown to users aged 18 or older who choose to enable them and complete any required consent steps. You can turn them off in Settings. Watching ads does not award coins.
