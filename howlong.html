Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. In short

HowLong? has no accounts and no servers of ours. We do not collect, store or transmit your personal information. The only company that receives anything is Google, and only so that the ads can be shown.

2. What stays on your device

The dates you save (title, date, emoji) and your app settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us, and removing the app removes them.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising ID, its IP address, general device information such as the model and the operating system version, and how you interact with an ad. Google handles that data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The advertising ID permission the app declares (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) exists for this and nothing else.

4. What the app does not do

There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no other tracking library in the app. We do not sell your data, we do not build a profile of you, and we have no way to tell one person from another.

5. Sharing a card

When you share a card, the image and the text are handed to the app you pick, such as a messenger or a mail app. Nothing passes through us. What happens to it after that is covered by that app's own policy.

6. Permissions

The app uses internet and network access to load ads, plus the advertising ID described above. It never asks for contacts, photos, files, location, the microphone or the camera.

7. How long anything is kept

Saved dates and settings stay on your device until you delete them in My dates or uninstall the app. We hold nothing, so there is nothing on our side to delete or to ask us for.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your choices

You can delete individual saved dates in My dates, reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings, limit personalised ads in those same settings, or uninstall the app to remove every trace of local data.

10. Languages, changes and contact

This policy is published in every language the app speaks, in the app and at https://gkimmulti-ui.github.io/howlong.html. The translations are meant to say the same thing; where one reads differently, the English version is the reference. If the policy changes, the new version appears in both places. For any question about privacy, open Settings and then Contact us, or write to gkimmulti@gmail.com.
