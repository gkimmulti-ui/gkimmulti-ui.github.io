Turn your phone into a bright, customizable scrolling LED sign

Light LED turns your smartphone into a bright and easy-to-use LED message board.

    Google Play · Good to know

    Light LED turns your smartphone into a bright and easy-to-use LED message board. Enter any message and display it as a large scrolling sign. Use it at shops, events, concerts, airports, meetings, parties, or anywhere you need to communicate clearly from a distance. Main features • Create custom scrolling LED messages • Choose from dot matrix, neon, and segment styles • Change text colors, backgrounds, fonts, and visual effects • Adjust scrolling direction, speed, brightness, and text size • Use ready-made templates such as OPEN, CLOSED, SALE, WELCOME, and THANK YOU • Keep the screen on while displaying your message • Use maximum screen brightness for better visibility • Save frequently used messages for quick access • Choose between tabbed editing and a large-preview layout • Supports multiple languages Create a message, customize its appearance, and tap Play to display it in full-screen mode. Light LED is useful for: • Store signs and promotional messages • Meeting someone at an airport or station • Concerts, festivals, and sporting events • Parties, celebrations, and birthday messages • Guiding visitors or sharing short announcements • Communicating in noisy environments Your messages and presets are stored on your device unless otherwise stated in the app’s privacy policy. Make your message bright, clear, and easy to notice with Light LED.
