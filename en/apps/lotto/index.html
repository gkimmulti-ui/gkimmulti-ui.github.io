    Google Play · Good to know

    "I Want to Win the Lotto Too" is a lottery number helper app that allows you to easily and enjoyably generate Lotto 6/45 numbers, save your purchased numbers by draw number, and even check the winning results. Create numbers quickly with a single button, or generate your own unique number combinations by setting your desired conditions. By saving your purchased numbers, you can conveniently check the number of matching numbers and winning results by comparing them with the latest winning numbers. ■ Various Number Generation Methods • Fast Automatic Number Generation Conveniently generate 6 Lotto numbers with a single button. • Balanced Recommended Numbers Creates balanced number combinations by considering odd and even numbers, number ranges, and more. • Custom Conditional Numbers You can set your own conditions by including the numbers you want and excluding the ones you do not. • Semi-Automatic Number Generation You select some numbers yourself, and the app automatically completes the rest. • Simultaneous Generation for Multiple Games You can generate numbers for multiple games at once by selecting your desired number of games: 1, 3, or 5. ■ Saving and Managing Purchased Numbers You can save generated numbers or numbers you have personally selected as purchased numbers. • Save numbers by draw • Differentiate between purchased numbers and favorites • Manage fixed number combinations • Edit and delete saved numbers • Memo and manage by number • Safely save within your smartphone ■ Conveniently Checking Winning Results You can check results by comparing your saved numbers with the announced Lotto winning numbers. • Check the latest winning numbers • Check the bonus number • Highlight matching numbers • Check the number of matching numbers • Winning rank information • Manage results by draw • Manually refresh new results When using the auto-check feature, the app checks for new winning results within the limits allowed by the operating system and notifies you via notification. The time of verification may be delayed depending on your device's battery settings or background execution restrictions. ■ Easy and Friendly Design We have minimized complex features and designed the app so that anyone can use it easily. Through colorful Lotto balls and intuitive menus, you can conveniently use the app for everything from generating numbers to saving and checking results. ■ Main Menu • Home: Check the current draw and recommended numbers • Create Numbers: Generate numbers using various methods • My Numbers: Manage saved and purchased numbers • Check Winnings: Compare your numbers with the latest winning numbers • Settings: Manage automatic checks and notification features ■ User Guide The numbers provided in this app are reference combinations created through random generation or based on conditions selected by the user. We do not predict the probability of a specific number winning or guarantee a win. Please purchase Lotto tickets within a reasonable range based on your own judgment and responsibility.
