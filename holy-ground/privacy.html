HOLY GROUND PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: August 1, 2026

1. OVERVIEW

Holy Ground is developed by Kenneth App. The application helps users record scripture study, Come, Follow Me study, personal study time, temple visits, ordinances, D-Day events, and widget preferences.

2. DATA STORED ON THE DEVICE

By default, private records are stored locally on the user's device. These may include study completion, study time, scripture ranges, personal notes, temple visit dates, temple names, ordinance categories, optional person labels, and D-Day events.

The weekly study view summarizes records already stored on the device and does not upload study notes. Your personal verse card stores its text, reference, and color choice in encrypted storage on this device. Holy Ground does not share the card or include it in its backups. The existing optional, user-initiated encrypted Google Drive backup for study records is unchanged. On Android, Read Aloud uses an installed offline voice to read the displayed scripture. It does not send the text to a speech service or read your study notes.

3. HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS

Widgets receive only the minimum information needed for display. Personal notes and ordinance person names are not intentionally displayed in widgets.

4. BACKUPS

Users can create password-protected backup files. Holy Ground does not store or recover the backup password. Anyone with the backup file and password may be able to access its contents.

A backup can be kept as a file on the device or uploaded to the user's own Google Drive. A Drive backup is encrypted on the device with the user's backup password before it is uploaded, so the stored file cannot be read without that password, by Google, by Kenneth App, or by anyone else. Uploads go to the application data folder, a private area of the user's Drive that only Holy Ground can see; the app cannot list, read or change any other file in that Drive. Signing in shows Holy Ground the Google account's email address and name, so that the connected account can be displayed. The user can disconnect the account or delete every Drive backup from Settings at any time.

5. CHURCH WEBSITES

Temple scheduling and Church news links open official Church websites. Holy Ground does not collect or store Church account passwords, reservation credentials, or official website login sessions.

6. NETWORK ACCESS

Network access is used to retrieve publicly available Church news and optional remote configuration, and — only when the user asks for it — to upload or download the user's own encrypted backups to their own Google Drive. Private study and temple records are never sent to a server operated by Kenneth App; there is no such server.

6-1. Prayer list
Prayers you write are stored on this device only. They are not sent to any Kenneth App server, are not visible to other users, and there is no shared board where anyone else can read them. A prayer can name health or family matters, which data protection law treats as sensitive, so the app does not take it off the device. Sharing opens your device's own share sheet: you choose the app and the recipient, and what happens after that is governed by the app you chose.

7. DIAGNOSTICS

The app may keep a small local diagnostic log for unexpected errors. It is designed not to include private study notes, ordinance person names, or temple visit details. Users can view, copy, and clear the log.

8. DATA DELETION

Users can delete all private records from Settings. Deleted information may remain in backups the user created earlier, including any kept in their Google Drive; those can be removed from the Google Drive backup screen.

9. CHILDREN

The app is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated server.

10. CONTACT

Questions about this policy or about a personal record can be sent to Kenneth App at gkimmulti@gmail.com.
