Small savings. A city of your own.

Log what you saved on coffee, delivery, transport and more to earn coins and XP. Build and upgrade nine kinds of buildings as your town grows through six city levels. Category stats, streaks, achievements and optional daily reminders help you keep the habit going.

  • 9 building types
  • 6 city levels
  • Savings stats
  • No sign-up
Good to know

Savings are entered manually. The game does not connect to bank accounts or hold or transfer money. Its coins and buildings have no monetary value.
