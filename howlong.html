Privacy policy — updated 2026-11-06 1. Developer and local data Kenneth App provides HowLong?. No app account is required. Dates, titles, emoji, favorites and settings are stored in the app's local storage; we do not operate a server that receives these entries. Your Android or iOS backup and device-transfer settings may allow the operating system to back up and restore app data. We do not control those platform backups. 2. Advertising and consent SDKs The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on SDK configuration, consent and available signals, Google may collect or share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising/app/device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and performance/diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service; this does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no diagnostics. Google processes data under https://policies.google.com/privacy. Ad requests are gated by UMP's canRequestAds result. When required, Settings provides advertising privacy options. A test-ad build is not a promise that SDK network communication is disabled. 3. Permissions and reminders Internet/network access and advertising-related permissions support Google SDKs. Notifications are optional: permission is requested when you enable date reminders. Restart handling restores scheduled reminders. SDK dependencies can declare wake-lock or foreground-service permissions for their background work; HowLong? does not use these to track your activity. The app does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs, broad photo/file access, microphone, camera or accessibility-service access. 4. Sharing, deletion and retention A shared card or text is sent to the app you select, whose privacy policy then applies. Delete saved dates in My dates or clear app storage/uninstall to remove local app data. Restored platform backups may restore previously saved entries; manage backups through your device/account settings. Google and recipient apps determine retention of data they receive under their own policies. Contact us about app privacy; we cannot delete data controlled by those providers on your behalf. 5. Audience and contact HowLong? is not directed at children under 13. An entered birthday is a calculation input, not verification of the person using the app. Changes will be reflected in the app and published policy.
