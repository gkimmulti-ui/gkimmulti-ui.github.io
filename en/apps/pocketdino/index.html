Pocket Dino

Meet a little dinosaur on your Android screen with encouraging captions, weather, tasks and reminders.

    Google Play · Good to know

    Pocket Dino is a little dinosaur companion for your Android screen. Let it wander above other apps and greet you with expressions and on-screen captions. Choose your companion Meet Tyrannosaurus, Triceratops, Brachiosaurus, Stegosaurus, Ankylosaurus and Parasaurolophus. Discover smiles, winks, angry faces, cheering and a wagging tail. Walk, run and hear your name in captions Tap your dinosaur for a reaction or drag it to a different spot. Choose free roaming, horizontal or vertical movement, a specific compass direction, or staying still. Adjust speed and walking style. Add a name or nickname for personalized captions. Voice conversations and free-text AI chat are not included. A few kind words along the way Enjoy 300 random messages: 297 combinations of original encouragement and companion responses, plus three classic quotations. Set how often your dinosaur speaks, turn captions on or off, and use focus mode or quiet nights. Weather and everyday helpers Allow location access to check your local forecast, or search for a city. Location is checked while using the app, with no background tracking. A focus timer, task list and reminders help with small daily routines. An easy start with battery controls An illustrated guide introduces the app. Choose walking or running and an economy mode. Dinosaur animation stops when the screen is off or locked. Actual battery use depends on your device and settings. Before you start Displaying the dinosaur above other apps requires overlay permission. Notifications need notification permission. Local weather optionally uses location permission; weather and city search need internet access. Weather data: MET Norway. Reminders may be delayed by device settings; reopen the app after restarting your device to restore schedules. Available in Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German and Indonesian.
