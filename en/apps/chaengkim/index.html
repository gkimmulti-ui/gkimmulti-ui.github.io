    📌 All the things in your life that are easy to forget—'Chamgim' is all you need. Insurance maturity dates, savings payment due dates, subscription billing dates, medication times, regular car checkups, even your parents' birthdays… There are simply too many to keep track of just in your head. Chamgim is a lifestyle management app that gathers scattered schedules and information in one place and reminds you of important moments with notifications before they arrive. ■ Perfect for You · Those who tend to forget things because there are so many to keep track of · Those who don't want to miss recurring payments like insurance, subscriptions, or utility bills · Those who want to manage family schedules and information as well · Those who want to neatly organize scattered memos and records in one app ■ Key Features 🔔 Smart Notifications — Receive push notifications in advance for due dates, payment dates, and anniversaries; set custom alarms and check past alarms 📋 Management — Manage recurring expenses and contracts such as telecom, subscriptions, memberships, insurance, loans, savings, deposits, accounts, utilities, vehicles, real estate, certifications, mileage, gift certificates, etc. 🌿 Lifestyle — Medication, doctor visits, exercise, weight, household budget, refrigerator, cleaning, appliances, pets, plants, parenting, recipes, wardrobe, packages, travel, goals, and daily logs 🚗 Real-time Timer (Parking & Storage) — Calculates elapsed time and estimated charges in real-time; end times are saved as 'Records' Preservation 👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Acquaintance Management — Separate management of items by profile + Emergency contact list 🔒 Secure Security — PIN/biometric authentication app lock, storage of SNS/app account/door lock passwords ☁️ Cloud Backup & Restore — Secure backup via Google login, restore when changing devices 🌙 Convenient Usability — Dark mode, photo attachment, category organization ■ Try using it like this · "Car insurance expires in 3 months" → Receive reminders in advance and compare renewals · "Subscription payments on the 25th of every month" → Clean up unnecessary subscriptions · "Take medicine twice a day" → Don't forget to take it · "Underground parking lot B2-14" → Find it instantly without getting lost Your life is complicated; now, Chaegim will remember for you.
